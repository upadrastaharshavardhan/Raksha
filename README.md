# 🌸 What is Raksha?

**Raksha** is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** designed as a modern **Digital Rakhi**.

The idea is simple:

> A brother should be able to gift more than just a Rakhi.
> He should also be able to gift a small layer of digital protection. 🛡️

Raksha provides quick-access emergency tools, trusted contacts, location-based SOS messaging, voice activation, shake detection, safety utilities, and more.

It can be installed directly on an Android or iPhone home screen and works like a lightweight app.

### 🔐 Built with Privacy at the Core

Raksha does **not** require:

* ❌ User accounts
* ❌ Sign-ups
* ❌ Cloud storage
* ❌ A backend server
* ❌ Tracking
* ❌ Paid subscriptions

Your information remains stored **locally on your device**.

# 🚨 Emergency SOS

Raksha V6 provides **three ways to activate an emergency SOS**.

## 1️⃣ Shake to SOS 📳

Shake your phone to trigger the emergency workflow.

Useful when opening the app or navigating through the interface is difficult.

---

## 2️⃣ Secret Voice Phrase 🎙️

Create your own custom emergency phrase.

For example:

> **"Bhairava, help me"**

When configured and supported by the browser/device, Raksha detects the phrase and starts the SOS workflow.

The phrase can be customised by the user.

---

## 3️⃣ Hold the SOS Button 🛑

Press and hold the large emergency button for approximately **2 seconds**.

This helps reduce accidental SOS activations while keeping the emergency trigger simple.

# ⚡ Instant Primary Contact SOS

One of the most important features of Raksha is the **Primary Contact system**.

During setup, the user adds trusted contacts to the **Trusted Circle**.

The first or selected important contact can be configured as the:

## ⭐ Primary Contact

When SOS is triggered:

```text
🚨 SOS Triggered
       ↓
📍 Current location requested
       ↓
💬 Emergency message prepared
       ↓
📱 Primary Contact's WhatsApp opened
       ↓
📨 User taps Send
```

The message can include:

* 🚨 Emergency alert
* 📍 Current/live location link
* 👤 Identity information configured by the user
* 🆘 Request for immediate help

### Why Primary Contact?

During a real emergency, a person may only be able to perform **one or two actions**.

Instead of manually selecting multiple contacts, Raksha focuses first on the most important person.

> **Less navigation. Less decision-making. Faster access to help.**

Secondary trusted contacts remain available when there is enough time.

# 👥 Trusted Circle

Build a personal emergency network.

Raksha allows users to maintain a **Trusted Circle of up to 7 contacts**.

Each contact can be used for:

* 📱 WhatsApp emergency alerts
* 💬 SMS access where supported
* 🆘 Quick emergency communication

### Recommended Setup

```text
⭐ Primary Contact
   └── Parent / Brother / Husband / Closest Trusted Person

👥 Secondary Contacts
   ├── Family Member
   ├── Friend
   ├── Relative
   └── Trusted Neighbour
```

Choose people who are likely to respond quickly.

# 📲 Installation Guide

Raksha is a **Progressive Web App**.

No app store download is required.

## 🤖 Android

1. Open Raksha using **Google Chrome**
2. Open the browser menu
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** or **Install App**
4. Confirm installation
5. Raksha will appear on your home screen

## 🍎 iPhone

1. Open Raksha using **Safari**
2. Tap the **Share** button
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**
4. Confirm
5. Raksha will appear like an app on your home screen

# ⚙️ One-Time Safety Setup

For the best experience, complete the following setup.

### Step 1 — Install Raksha

Add Raksha to your device home screen.

### Step 2 — Create Your Trusted Circle

Go to:

```text
Circle → Add Trusted Contacts
```

Add the people you trust most.

### Step 3 — Set a Primary Contact

Select the person who should be contacted first during an emergency.

⭐ **This is your most important emergency contact.**

### Step 4 — Enable Shake SOS

Go to the home screen and enable:

```text
📳 Shake to SOS
```

Allow motion permissions if your browser asks.

### Step 5 — Configure Your Voice Phrase

Go to:

```text
Voice → Set Secret Phrase
```

Example:

> **"Bhairava, help me"**

Choose a phrase that is:

* Easy to remember
* Unlikely to be spoken accidentally
* Recognisable to you

### Introduced

* Big red SOS button
* Hold-to-trigger interaction

* Fake incoming call
* Emergency siren
* Flashlight
* Safety Check-In timer
* Emergency helplines
* Period and wellness tracker
* Safety tips

* Add to Home Screen

### Limitation

SOS could prepare information for sharing, but the emergency communication flow still required too many manual actions.

---

## 🎙️ V2 — Custom Voice Trigger

**Goal:** Introduce hands-free emergency activation.

### Added

* Custom emergency voice phrase
* User-defined secret trigger
* Voice Guard ON/OFF
* Suggested phrases
* Improved privacy-focused voice handling

### Limitation

The actual sharing flow still depended heavily on device and browser behaviour.

On some devices, especially iPhones, the generic share sheet did not reliably direct users to the intended Trusted Circle contacts.

---

## 💬 V3 — Multi-Contact WhatsApp SOS

**Goal:** Alert multiple trusted people.

### Added

* Multi-contact WhatsApp emergency flow
* Progress feedback
* Emergency message preparation
* Location sharing
* Clipboard backup for emergency messages
* Clearer emergency controls

### Limitation

The user still needed to manually interact with multiple WhatsApp chats and tap **Send**.

---

## 📱 V4 — Better Contact Handling

**Goal:** Improve targeting of Trusted Circle contacts.

### Added

* Direct WhatsApp contact links
* `wa.me` based flows
* WhatsApp app link support where available
* Individual WhatsApp actions

* Clearer Trusted Circle contact panel

### Limitation

In a serious emergency, switching between multiple contacts may still be too slow.

# ⚠️ Important Web App Limitations

Raksha is intentionally built as a **pure web application/PWA**.

This keeps it:

* Free
* Easy to share
* Easy to install
* No app-store approval required
* No backend required

However, web browsers and mobile operating systems impose security restrictions.

## A web app cannot guarantee:

* Automatic SMS sending
* SMS sending while the phone is locked
* Background operation in every situation
* Continuous microphone access in the background
* Guaranteed shake detection when the browser is suspended
* Bypassing WhatsApp's requirement for user interaction

For this reason, Raksha should be considered a **safety companion and quick-access emergency tool**, not a replacement for local emergency services or a guaranteed emergency communication system.

---

# 🧩 Recommended Safety Companion

For users who need capabilities that require a native Android application, such as deeper background emergency functionality, consider also using an appropriate open-source native emergency app.

One option mentioned during the development of Raksha is:

**SOS Alerter** — available through F-Droid.

Raksha can serve as the easy-to-install **Digital Rakhi safety companion**, while a native emergency application may provide additional device-level capabilities that a browser-based PWA cannot access.

> Always review an application's current features, permissions, device compatibility, and reliability before relying on it in an emergency.
